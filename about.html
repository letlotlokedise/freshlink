About FreshLink Trade Hub

FreshLink Trade Hub is a digital marketplace platform founded by young Batswana to bridge the gap between local service providers and urban customers in Greater Gaborone.

📍 About Us

We are a youth-led platform connecting small-scale farmers, carpenters, and transporters with customers in Gaborone and surrounding areas.


🎯 Our Mission

To formalise the informal economy, reduce youth unemployment, and create sustainable income opportunities through technology.

🌟 Our Vision

To become the leading digital marketplace in Botswana that empowers youth entrepreneurs and strengthens the local economy.

💎 Our Values

  • ✅ Transparency
  • ✅ Fair Trade
  • ✅ Innovation
  • ✅ Community Empowerment
  • ✅ Sustainability
